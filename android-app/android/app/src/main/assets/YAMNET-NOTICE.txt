Heting uses this general audio-event classifier as a genre hint. Rock/Folk scores
are not guaranteed musical genre accuracy, and model confidence is not a measured
accuracy percentage. Three consistent confident windows are required. Otherwise
Heting retains the original sound or the user's manual setting. Processing is
local to decoded playback audio; no microphone, recording or audio upload.
